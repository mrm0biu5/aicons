AIcons

A magic trick for iPhone

A spectator picks a card. Your phone sits on the table showing an ordinary home screen. Wave the card over it and the apps quietly rearrange themselves to spell it out: FIVE / OF / HEARTS, in real app icons.

How it works

  • Set a screenshot of your own home screen as the background, so it looks like your phone.
  • Lay the phone face up. When it's ready, the clock's colon turns red.
  • Hold the card over the phone. The front camera reads it, and after a moment the icons fade into the card's name.

Everything happens on the phone. Nothing is uploaded. Privacy policy.
